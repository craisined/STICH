A machine-learning project

Turn humming into classical music—and back.

STICH is an AI that translates between two musical worlds: the tune you hum and a richer classical rendition of it. Hum a melody, hear it reimagined. Play a classical phrase, hear how someone might hum it.

What is it?

Think of a translator — but for music.

When you translate a sentence from English to Spanish, the meaning stays the same but the words change. STICH does the same thing with music. It takes a simple hummed melody and re-voices it in a classical style — and it can go the other direction too.

The clever part: nobody handed it matched examples. We never showed it "this exact hum equals this exact classical piece." It learned the style of each world on its own and figured out how to travel between them.

How it works

One map of sound, and a step across it.

🗺

The map

An autoencoder learns to boil any clip down to a short list of numbers — a point on a map of sound — and to rebuild the audio from that point again.

📍

The two landmarks

Thousands of hums land in one region of that map, thousands of classical clips in another. Average each pile and you get a landmark for "sounds hummed" and one for "sounds classical."

➕

The step

Find your clip on the map, step away from the humming landmark and the same distance toward the classical one, then rebuild the audio from where you land.

In machine-learning terms: an autoencoder over raw audio plus vector arithmetic on its latent embeddings — subtract the mean embedding of one domain, add the mean of the other, decode. No adversarial training, and still no paired examples.

Under the hood

For the curious.

Model
Autoencoder over raw audio; style transfer is vector arithmetic on its latent embeddings.
Humming data
HumTrans — thousands of recorded hummed melodies.
Classical data
MusicNet — hundreds of classical recordings.
Audio format
Mono 10-second clips — 16 kHz on disk, resampled to 48 kHz for the model, which returns 48 kHz.
Training
PyTorch, multi-GPU (DDP) with mixed precision.
This site
Plain HTML, CSS & JavaScript, with a Flask server for the model.
